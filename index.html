Quantum computing has reached an inflection point. LIFT 2027, a Planckian conference hosted by the Scuola Normale Superiore, brings together an international group of scientists, spanning academia and industry, to discuss the most pressing challenges and recent advances on the road to fault-tolerant quantum computing.
