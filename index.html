LIFT 2027 Leaning Into Fault Tolerance

Dates
22–26 March 2027
Venue
Scuola Normale Superiore, Pisa
Organised by

Quantum computing has reached an inflection point. At LIFT 2027, Planckian brings together an international group of scientists, spanning academia and industry, to discuss the most pressing challenges and recent advances on the road to fault-tolerant quantum computing.
