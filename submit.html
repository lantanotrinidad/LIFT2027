LIFT 2027 is organised by Planckian S.r.l. (VAT 02423890504), Lungarno Galileo Galilei 13, 56125 Pisa (PI), Italy, the controller of the personal data you give on this form: your name, email, affiliation, position, and the details of your contribution, including co-author names.

We use it to review your abstract, put together the programme, publish accepted abstracts in the book of abstracts and on the conference website, and contact you about your submission and the conference. This is necessary to handle the submission you are making (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR): the required fields are needed for that, and we cannot consider a submission without them. We delete submissions 12 months after the conference, except abstracts published in the book of abstracts. If you sign up for news, we keep your name and email until you unsubscribe.

You can ask to access, correct or delete your data, restrict or object to how we use it, or receive a copy of it, and you can withdraw your consent at any time, by writing to {{ primaryEmail }}.
